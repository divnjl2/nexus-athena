# ADR-0028: Ask for alternatives that challenge the oracle

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-28
- Date scope: format and rationale review; the original decision predates this review

### Historical authoring notes
Status: accepted after the bounded Seaborn calibration reviewer assay.

## Context
The original reviewer accepted a refactoring of the proposed formatting strategy.
It did not challenge a candidate assertion demanding the literal 1e6. Native audit
withheld publication for known gaps; independent benchmark scoring failed.

## Decision
A separate post-evaluation assay retained the exact candidate and native policy,
but requested a materially different mechanism and a different valid representation
where the issue permits them. The reviewer proposed full numeric labels without
scientific abbreviation and a different ordering of the scaled representation.
Native audit rejected the candidate for failing the first declared valid control.
Its separate violation probe errored and remained inconclusive. The assay used one
new model request and four test runs; aggregate lineage costs were 12 and 10.

Promote these instructions, including scrutiny of literals and internal-state
assertions against the public issue. Preserve real knowledge gaps, activation
independence, execution typing and acceptance policy. Request at most two valid
controls and one violation per clause within existing shared budgets.

## Consequences
This is an instruction, not a semantic-diversity verifier. Validity remains an
owner declaration; reviewers can still supply weak alternatives. The assay was
designed after failed calibration evaluations, is not a blind performance study,
and does not change old results or establish a general gain.
